# Arabic terms

Working glossary for Arabic output. Arabic project terminology differs between countries, employers and translations, so two common forms are given where usage is split. Follow the person's own usage when they show a preference, and show the English term in brackets on first use.

This list is a practical starting point, not an official lexicon. For training material or exam preparation, check terms against the certification body's own Arabic glossary.
